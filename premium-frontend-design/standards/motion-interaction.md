# Motion and Interaction Standard

## Motion purpose

Motion should communicate:

- Hierarchy
- State change
- Spatial relationship
- Continuity
- Progress
- Brand character

Do not animate only to make a page look active.

## Motion hierarchy

Use different intensities for:

1. Micro interaction
2. Component transition
3. Section motion
4. Page-level transition

The larger the motion scope, the more justification it needs.

## Preferred motion qualities

- Natural easing
- Controlled spring behavior
- Short feedback loops
- Stagger when revealing meaningful groups
- Smooth enter / exit continuity

## Marketing techniques

When appropriate:

- Typography reveal
- Image mask reveal
- Layered parallax
- Sticky narrative transition
- Product animation
- Interactive background
- Cursor-responsive element

## Product techniques

Prefer subtle:

- Fade
- Translate
- Scale within a narrow range
- Height / opacity expansion when layout-safe
- Drawer / dialog transitions
- Toast feedback

## Hover behavior

Avoid generic `scale(1.1)` on every card.

Better hover responses may include:

- Border contrast
- Background shift
- Controlled translation
- Icon movement
- Media crop change
- Text-color hierarchy
- Subtle lighting response

## Reduced motion

Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Reduce or remove:

- Parallax
- Scroll-linked effects
- Large transforms
- Non-essential autoplay motion

Functional feedback should remain clear.
